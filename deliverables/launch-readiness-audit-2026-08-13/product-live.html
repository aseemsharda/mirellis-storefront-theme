How this came to exist

Nothing here began as a product. It began with an observation.

Product record 003 · under continuing reviewStillness is an optional sensory instrument inside an evening ritual.

The product began with a practical question: could scent and touch make the beginning of an evening ritual more tangible for someone who wants a physical cue?

That question does not establish a neurological mechanism or guarantee a change in state. Dimmed light, temperature, breath, sound, touch and scent are all possible ritual elements; a product is not required.

Mirellis chose an externally applied aromatic oil because it is portable, screen-free and repeatable. The public evidence register keeps this design rationale separate from scientific proof and from classical Ayurvedic tradition.

The practice we chose

Aromatic temple oil — a classical evening practice, honestly bounded.

Classical Ayurvedic tradition

The external application of aromatic oils to the head and temples, and the inhalation of aromatic preparations, are described in the classical Ayurvedic evening routine (ratricharya). Tradition associates them with grounding, calming, and preparing the body for rest.

That is what tradition describes. It is not the same as modern clinical proof, and we will not pretend it is. We keep tradition and evidence separate on purpose — and where we cannot yet point to strong evidence, we would rather say nothing than overstate. That restraint is the honest version of an Ayurvedic ritual, and we think it is the only one worth making.

Who this is for — and who it isn't
It may suit you if

You end the day wired, rely on screens to wind down, or want a clear line between work and rest.

It is not for

It is not intended for young children; for anyone with a known allergy to tree nuts (it contains sweet almond oil) or to any of its botanicals; or for use on broken or irritated skin. It is not a sedative, sleep medication, or treatment for any condition. If you are pregnant, nursing, or managing a health condition, please speak with your doctor first.

Using it safely

External use only — not to be swallowed. Applied to the skin (temples, behind the ears, wrists) and inhaled. Patch-test first; keep away from the eyes and mucous membranes.

Every one of these is here to return control to you — so nothing about the ritual is a surprise.

The ritual

Two to five minutes, at the end of the day — after screens, before bed.

  1. 1
    WarmA few drops of oil between the palms.
  2. 2
    ApplyTo the temples, behind the ears, and wrists — slow, unhurried circles.
  3. 3
    BreatheCup the hands near the face; three slow breaths. Let the shoulders drop.

The scent, the warmth, and the pressure are the same each evening. That sameness is the point: repetition is what turns a few minutes into a signal the body begins to read as rest.

Questions answered
Do I swallow it?+

No — it is for external use only, applied to the skin and inhaled.

How is this different from a pillow spray or an essential-oil roller?+

Stillness isn't an aromatherapy oil — the formulation is the delivery mechanism for a specific evening transition. It's chosen deliberately, fully disclosed, and honest about what we can and can't yet show.

Is this a sleep medication?+

No. It is not a sedative or a treatment for any condition; it is spoken about observationally. For medical concerns, see your doctor.

Will it make me drowsy, or affect driving?+

It is applied to the skin and inhaled, not ingested. It is a ritual cue, not a sedative.

When will I notice something?+

Honestly — there is no promised timeline. The ritual works through repetition; what people describe tends to be quiet, not dramatic.

Why are there never discounts?+

Because a considered product held to a fair, steady price is part of the trust — discounting would undercut it.
