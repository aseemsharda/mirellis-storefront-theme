# After Work Pilot — Production Pack

**Status:** origin pilot specification. Its exact screen sequence is superseded by `after-work-release-candidate-2026-08.md`; its private-companion, distinct-route, optional-media and qualitative-pilot boundaries remain governing. Not approved for production release until the current release gates pass.
**Purpose:** define one complete, testable Mirellis transition session before any second transition or content-library expansion.

## 1. Pilot premise

### The moment

A person has stopped working but does not feel that work has ended.

### The job

Help them make one visible end to a work period, begin one ordinary non-work action, and leave with no pressure to report a result.

### Non-claim

The session does not claim to induce recovery, regulate physiology, improve sleep, or resolve anxiety. It gives a person a bounded way to mark an ending and choose the next part of the day.

### Testable promise

> “In about ten minutes, you will make one clear ending to your work period and choose what comes next.”

## 2. Exact session flow

### Screen 1 — Today

**Headline:** “What brings you here today?”  
**Primary entry:** “Work is still with me”  
**Secondary:** “Explore other moments” (not shown in the pilot if no other complete service exists).  
**Privacy line:** “A private practice companion. No account required.”

### Screen 2 — Recognition

**Question:** “What is still with you after work?”  
**Support copy:** “Choose the closest sentence. It changes the first practice you receive.”

| Choice | Route key |
| --- | --- |
| An unfinished point is still replaying. | `open-loop` |
| I keep checking and cannot disengage. | `checking` |
| My body still feels switched on. | `activated` |
| I am not sure; I just have not switched off. | `unmarked-ending` |

**Exit:** “Leave for now”  
**Rule:** no fifth catch-all route, no “smaller version,” and no path labels.

### Screen 3 — Why this / start

The acknowledgment repeats only what was selected. It must not introduce a hidden interpretation.

| Route | Acknowledgement | Small promise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open loop | “An unfinished point can keep asking for attention after work has ended.” | “First, you will give tomorrow one clear place to begin.” |
| Checking | “Checking can keep a work window open after its useful part has finished.” | “First, you will create a little distance from the cue that keeps pulling you back.” |
| Activated | “Sometimes effort stays with the body after the task has ended.” | “First, you will make one safe change in the signals around you.” |
| Unmarked ending | “You do not have to explain what is happening before you can mark an ending.” | “First, you will move your attention to one ordinary thing that is actually next.” |

**Primary:** “Start this practice”  
**Exit:** “Leave for now”

### Screens 4–6 — The practice

The session should be designed as a quiet, timed sequence. The app is a companion; the real work happens outside it. The person may leave and later resume at the exact step without a completion or outcome being created.

| Time | Session moment | App state | Person’s action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:30 | First cue | One route-specific instruction | Completes the selected first cue below. |
| 1:30–3:00 | Make the ending visible | One shared instruction | Closes/puts away one work surface and changes one environmental signal. |
| 3:00–8:00 | Begin the evening | Quiet session state with optional Stillness handoff | Begins one ordinary non-work activity outside the app. |
| 8:00–10:00 | Return if useful | A simple return cue, never a forced countdown | Returns for an optional, honest check-in. |

### Route-specific first cue

#### Open loop — 0:00–1:30

**Heading:** “Leave a return point.”  
**Instruction:** “Write one plain line: the next visible action. Make it short enough that tomorrow-you will recognise it.”  
**Completion control:** “I left a return point.”

#### Checking — 0:00–1:30

**Heading:** “Create one piece of distance.”  
**Instruction:** “Turn over the phone, close the inbox, or move one work device out of reach. Keep genuinely urgent channels available through your normal way of receiving them.”  
**Completion control:** “I created some distance.”

#### Activated — 0:00–1:30

**Heading:** “Change one signal.”  
**Instruction:** “Let both feet meet the floor or let your back meet a chair. Then choose one safe change: drink water, loosen one layer, change posture, or step into another room.”  
**Completion control:** “I changed one signal.”

#### Unmarked ending — 0:00–1:30

**Heading:** “Find what is actually next.”  
**Instruction:** “Look around and name one ordinary thing that is waiting for you now: food, water, movement, a person, a shower, or quiet.”  
**Completion control:** “I know what is next.”

### Shared ending cue — 1:30–3:00

**Heading:** “Let the work surface close.”  
**Instruction:** “Close one work tab, document, or notebook. Then change one thing in the room: the light, your chair, the room itself, or where the phone is placed.”  
**Completion control:** “The work surface is closed.”

### Quiet transition environment — 3:00–8:00

**Heading:** “Begin the evening.”  
**Instruction:** “Choose one ordinary next action: make food or a drink, shower, walk, sit somewhere else, or spend a few quiet minutes with someone. Leave the app to begin it.”

**Optional support card, only if the Stillness playlist is confirmed:**

> **Open Stillness in Spotify**  
> A listening environment for the end of a workday. It opens in Spotify and is optional.

**Important implementation rule:** This is an external link, not an in-app player. The app announces “Stillness opened in a new tab. Nothing was saved.” It does not mark the person as having listened.

**Return cue:** “Return when it is useful. There is nothing more to complete right now.”

## 3. Check-in and close

### Check-in

**Heading:** “What is true now, if anything?”  
**Support copy:** “There is no correct result. Mirellis does not turn your response into a score.”

| Choice | Stored? | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I have a little more room. | Only after an explicit keep/save action. | Close with optional “Keep this practice for tomorrow.” |
| Partly enough for today. | Only after an explicit keep/save action. | Close with optional “Keep this practice for tomorrow.” |
| This approach did not fit today. | No record by default. | “Choose another approach” returns to the other three entry routes. |
| Nothing yet. | No record by default. | “Back to today.” No attempt to prolong the session. |

**Optional authored note:** “A private note, if you want one.”  
**Helper:** “Stored only on this device. Mirellis does not use it to interpret you.”

### Close

**Heading:** “You made a first choice.”  
**Body:** “That is enough for today.”  
**Primary:** “Back to today.”

**Only if outcome was explicitly positive/partly enough:**

**Secondary:** “Keep this practice for tomorrow.”  
**Visible result after tap:** “Saved. Tomorrow’s suggestion will build on this practice.”  
**Actual result:** Today presents “Return to this practice”; Personal Observatory displays the saved route immediately.

### Optional doors after a kept/completed session

These must be visually secondary and must never appear before the practical transition.

1. **Understand this moment** — opens one editorially reviewed Observatory Finding.
2. **A ritual tool for this transition** — opens Stillness Elixir only if the product-door decision is approved.
3. **Open Personal Observatory** — opens the person’s local record.

## 4. Film 01 production brief

### Title

**The Workday Needs an Ending**

### Job in the product

Offer optional context for someone who asks why Mirellis makes such a small, deliberate ending visible. The film must not be required before or during the session.

### Duration and placement

- 3–5 minutes.
- Available after the session under “Understand this moment.”
- On the website, it may lead to the After Work route. On YouTube, its primary description link points directly to the route.

### Narrative spine

1. Work can stop externally while attention remains occupied.
2. Modern life often has no clear signal that one chapter has closed.
3. Mirellis studies this boundary as a transition, not as a personal failure.
4. A deliberate return point, work-surface close, environmental shift, and ordinary next action can make the boundary visible.
5. The invitation: “Try the After Work transition.”

### Required language constraints

- No “fix,” “heal,” “reset your nervous system,” “regulate,” “cure,” “guarantee,” or clinical promise.
- No biometrics, scores, stock imagery of medical devices, or simulated research results.
- Do not imply that the Stillness Elixir is necessary or that it changes a person’s condition.
- All research statements must be reviewed against the selected Observatory Finding and cited in the publication description where relevant.

### Production format

- A quiet, original Mirellis film: work surface, threshold, light shift, hands moving an object, a door/window/room change, an ordinary evening action.
- No autoplay in the app or on the site.
- Captions, transcript, and descriptive title are mandatory.

## 5. Finding handoff editorial brief

### Product label

“Understand this transition”

### Required editorial framing

“The Observatory studies what can make an ending difficult to recognise. This is context for the practice you chose—not a claim about you or a promise that the practice will have a particular result.”

### Pre-publication checks

- One canonical Finding number, title, route name, and URL.
- Source references attached to every scientific/physiological statement.
- Claims qualified to match the evidence; no unqualified “requires,” “causes,” or duration claim without an approved source.
- Clear distinction between an Observatory observation, a hypothesis, and a consumer practice.
- Editorial owner approves copy; safety/claim owner approves any health-adjacent wording.

## 6. Pilot protocol

### Participant criteria

8–12 adults who say that leaving work is sometimes difficult. No diagnostic screening, clinical claims, or collection of sensitive health data.

### Consent boundary

The app remains local. Test feedback is collected separately with a short, clear consent form. The facilitator does not read private in-app notes, exported records, or local storage.

### Facilitated session

1. Ask participant to use the route on their own phone, with normal access to their environment.
2. Observe only interaction friction with consent; do not coach them toward a particular outcome.
3. After the session, ask the seven blueprint questions plus: “What would you have expected Mirellis to do next?”
4. Record anonymised facilitator notes and verbatim feedback only with consent.

### Success evidence

- People understand the product job without a model explanation.
- The four first cues feel distinct and plausible.
- People can leave/resume without losing their place or feeling penalised.
- People understand that sound, Findings, and product are optional.
- At least one route feels meaningfully useful enough for a participant to choose to save it; absence of this outcome is also valid evidence and must not be disguised.

### Failure evidence

- People describe the route as a content feed, course, or generic meditation.
- The first cue feels like advice unrelated to the selected situation.
- The app asks for too many confirmations before the person does anything real.
- The product door feels like the reason the route exists.
- Participants cannot explain what gets saved or why.

## 7. Build acceptance criteria

The pilot build may be deployed only after:

- All four routes use the exact script above or an approved revision.
- The Stillness control is hidden unless the playlist is confirmed public and approved.
- The film and Finding doors are hidden until their asset records are approved.
- No complete state is created from “Nothing yet” or “This approach did not fit.”
- The practice can be left and resumed without silent progression.
- Save, completion, external-link, and deletion feedback are in accessible live regions.
- A first-time mobile user, keyboard-only user, and screen-reader user can complete each route.
- No internal IDs, module terminology, scores, streaks, generic “continue,” or invented Observatory entries appear.

## 8. Explicit decisions carried forward

For this prototype specification, the working defaults are:

- Private local practice companion: **yes**.
- Observatory-connected learning instrument: **no**.
- Product door: **defer until after the qualitative pilot**.
- Stillness asset: **pending confirmation**; no integration until approved.
- Film 01: **required before a film control ships**, not before the first sound-only pilot.
- App expansion beyond After Work: **blocked until pilot review**.
