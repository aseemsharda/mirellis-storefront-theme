# After Work release candidate — August 2026

Status: `preflight`. This document reconciles the current build with the earlier prototype pack; it does not certify the release.

## Supported public product

After Work has one immediate action route and one optional private 21-day programme:

1. A person selects one of four explicit situations.
2. The immediate practice gives four situation-specific actions and advances only after confirmation.
3. Completion stores no private record by default.
4. The person may leave, use optional sound or understanding, or deliberately begin the 21-day programme.
5. The programme gives one three-part practice per day, preserves an unfinished draft, records only chosen and confirmed actions locally, and has no score, streak, diagnosis or inferred progress.
6. A calendar file is optional and delivered by the person's calendar application; Mirellis does not claim to send an alarm.

## Canonical consumer scope

- One live app transition: After Work.
- Four explicit entry situations: unfinished point, repeated checking, physical activation, and an evening that has not begun.
- One immediate practice per selected situation.
- One optional 21-day Workday Exit programme.
- One local private record.
- Optional Spotify, public guide and Stillness Elixir doors; none is required.

No other app transition, Observatory contribution, account, push notification, research donation or private analytics is part of this release candidate.

## Gates before deployment approval

- Automated type, unit and production-build tests pass.
- Every immediate route and the Day 1 programme path pass keyboard and mobile browser checks.
- A named human records screen-reader, text-scaling, contrast, touch-target and reduced-motion evidence.
- The 8–12-person qualitative pilot produces an explicit continue, revise or stop decision.
- Claim and URL safeguards are approved for the Shopify development theme.
- Checkout, fulfilment, refund, email and rollback evidence is recorded.

Until all gates are verified, this is suitable for controlled testing—not mass acquisition or a “world-class” claim.
