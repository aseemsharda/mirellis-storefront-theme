# 001 — Dedicated app surface

- Date: 2026-08-04
- Decision: Mirellis Transition Platform V1 will run as a dedicated web app on a Mirellis subdomain and be linked from Shopify.
- Specification anchor: Platform specification §8.2; governance brief §4.1.
- Consequence: Shopify remains the storefront. The app owns the PWA manifest, service worker, local-first experience data, and application navigation.
- Status: Approved by product owner.
