# 002 — Preserve five-option self-guided cards

- Date: 2026-08-04
- Decision: Preserve five selectable options on SW-13, SW-14, WR-14 (Transition 002), DF-14, FR-14, RP-14, PW-14, and WR-14 (Transition 007) exactly as written in the transition-specific V1 specifications.
- Specification anchor: Platform specification §5.3 limits core screens to four selectable responses; the five named transition cards define five user-facing options.
- Rationale: Transition specifications are the source of truth for individual card content. The product owner explicitly approved retaining the full authored content.
- Contract impact: The shared content validator permits at most five options; no other card may exceed that limit.
- Status: Approved by product owner.
