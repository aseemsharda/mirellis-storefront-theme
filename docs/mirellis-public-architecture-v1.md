# Mirellis public architecture v2

Frozen as the Phase 2 development model on 19 August 2026. This replaces the earlier V1 public-navigation model; it does not change the app curriculum or publish new claims.

## One system, three levels

### 1. Visitor problem doors

The first public choice remains ordinary language:

1. Sleep
2. Focus
3. Restoration

These are navigation doors, not diagnoses, research categories or claims that every difficulty belongs to one of three boxes.

### 2. Observatory territories

Mirellis currently publishes work in three institutional territories:

1. Morning beginning — Sleep → Wakefulness
2. Work ending — Work → Recovery
3. Evening quieting — Stimulation → Restoration

These are fields of inquiry. They remain distinct from the problem doors above.

### 3. App moments

The current app presents four practical moments:

1. Waking
2. Focus
3. After Work
4. Night

These are product experiences, not additional Observatory territories. The website must describe only what the current app actually exposes and must not infer progress, diagnosis or research participation from app use.

## Canonical public navigation

- **Start here:** the problem-first homepage.
- **Observatory:** the single public register at `/pages/the-observatory`.
- **Get help:** the problem chooser at `/pages/what-is-going-on`.
- **Contribute:** the invited-pilot gateway at `/pages/contact#contribute` until the governed studio is ready.
- **About:** identity, scope and institutional purpose at `/pages/about`.
- **Rituals:** a visually subordinate route to `/pages/transitions`.

Sleep, Focus and Restoration remain accessible from Start here and Get help rather than occupying the institutional navigation.

## Contribution boundary

Editorial contribution and anonymous observation are separate systems.

- Expert Commentary and Evidence Note require named authorship, sources, disclosures, review and an editorial decision.
- Raw observations are never treated as authored research and never publish automatically.
- The Phase 2 contribution gateway accepts invitation requests only. It is not an upload portal.
- The Phase 4 studio will own drafts, files, reviews, revisions, decisions and publication records.

## Privacy boundary

The app remains private and local-first unless a person enters a separate, explicit contribution process. App answers and private records are not Observatory submissions. A later research-contribution system requires explicit consent, provenance, withdrawal, export and deletion.

## Content hierarchy

- **Finding:** a numbered, bounded public conclusion with status and limitations.
- **Evidence Note:** a source-led synthesis supporting or challenging a defined claim.
- **Guide:** a practical explanation for a specific question.
- **Journal:** editorial context, reporting and institutional updates.
- **Expert Commentary:** a named external interpretation or argument.
- **Observation:** private or moderated source material; never an automatically indexed publication.
