# Mirellis public architecture v1

Frozen for the August 2026 public release.

## Public territories

Mirellis currently publishes work in three broad territories. These are fields of inquiry, not a diagnostic model and not a claim that three routes describe every person:

1. Morning beginning — sleep toward wakefulness.
2. Work ending — work toward recovery.
3. Evening quieting — stimulation toward restoration.

## Active consumer tool

The current public app contains one fully supported vertical slice: **After Work**. It helps a person identify the concrete reason work is still open, take one relevant action, optionally follow a 21-day practice, and retain a private local record.

The site must not imply that the other two public territories have equivalent app programmes until they are actually built and tested.

## Internal curriculum

The repository contains a four-transition curriculum model used by earlier V1 work. It remains an internal governed source and must not be presented as four currently available consumer tools. No additional transitions are to be invented or exposed without a product change.

## Privacy boundary

V1 is a private, local-first practice companion. Personal answers and practice records remain in the person's browser. Observatory participation is not part of the current app and must never be implied by a link, label, default, or background process.

Any future research contribution requires a separate system with explicit consent, provenance, withdrawal, export, and deletion.

## Public navigation jobs

- **Today:** help with a real moment now.
- **Library:** answer a specific question and teach.
- **Observatory:** show the status, evidence basis, limitations, and review date of public records.
- **Shop:** offer optional instruments connected to a particular ritual.
- **Private record:** show only what the person deliberately selected or confirmed.
