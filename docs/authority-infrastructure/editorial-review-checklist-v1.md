# Mirellis editorial review checklist v1

No public record, Evidence Note or Expert Commentary is ready until every applicable item is answered.

## Identity and scope

- [ ] Named accountable author or institutional author with responsible editor.
- [ ] Contribution roles recorded.
- [ ] Territory and output type are correct.
- [ ] The title does not outrun the body.

## Claims and sources

- [ ] Observation, interpretation, mechanism, intervention and product claims are separated.
- [ ] Every material factual claim has a verified source.
- [ ] Primary research or systematic review used where available.
- [ ] Source register states what each source does and does not support.
- [ ] Association is not written as causation.
- [ ] Study population, design and material limitations remain visible.

## Trust and safety

- [ ] No diagnosis, treatment promise or guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] Traditional knowledge and modern evidence are not blended.
- [ ] Funding, employment, ownership and other relevant interests disclosed.
- [ ] AI use disclosed; human accountability confirmed.
- [ ] No confidential submission or identifiable private observation is exposed.
- [ ] Copyright, quotation and permissions checked.

## Publication record

- [ ] Stable record number and anchor assigned.
- [ ] Version, publication date, review date and next review set.
- [ ] Review level labelled exactly; internal review is not called peer review.
- [ ] Human-readable citation generated and checked.
- [ ] Structured data validates.
- [ ] Correction route works.
- [ ] Mobile, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior verified.
