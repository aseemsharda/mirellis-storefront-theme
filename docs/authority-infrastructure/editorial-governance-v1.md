# Mirellis Observatory editorial governance v1

Status: development candidate

Owner: Mirellis Editorial

Effective date: 19 August 2026

Next policy review: 19 February 2027

This policy governs public Observatory records, Evidence Notes and Expert Commentary. It does not turn Mirellis into a medical journal, and it does not describe the private app record or future research participation.

## Public record types

- **Working observation:** a bounded description of something people have explicitly reported or Mirellis has directly documented. It does not establish prevalence, mechanism or cause.
- **Practice hypothesis:** a clearly named action and the evidence or reasoning that makes it worth testing. It is not proof that the Mirellis practice produces a health or performance outcome.
- **Product record:** formulation, use, safety and commercial-interest information about a Mirellis product. Traditional use, design rationale and modern scientific evidence remain separate.
- **Under review:** a record whose wording, source or status is being reassessed. It remains visible with the reason stated unless safety, privacy or legal obligations require temporary removal.
- **Retired:** a superseded or unsupported record preserved with a retirement notice and replacement link when one exists.

## Minimum publication record

Every public record must expose:

1. a stable record number and anchor URL;
2. title, territory, record type and version;
3. publication date, last review date and next review deadline;
4. accountable human or institutional author and named review level;
5. a bounded public statement;
6. evidence basis and explicit limitation;
7. source links with a note describing what each source does and does not support;
8. funding, commercial-interest, conflict and AI-use disclosures;
9. a human-readable citation; and
10. a route for questions, corrections or challenges.

## Evidence and source rules

- Prefer primary research and systematic reviews from identifiable publishers or repositories. Commentary can provide context but cannot be the sole support for a scientific claim.
- A source is not evidence for every sentence around it. The source register records the precise proposition it supports and what it does not support.
- Study design, population and important limitations must remain visible when they materially change interpretation.
- Association is not described as causation. A product-design rationale is not described as clinical evidence.
- Traditional knowledge is attributed as tradition and is not presented as equivalent to modern clinical evidence.
- Mirellis does not publish a numeric evidence score. Status and limitations must be readable in words.

## Authorship, review and disclosure

- Every output has an accountable human author or an accountable institutional author with a clearly named responsible editorial function.
- External contributors describe their contribution using the relevant CRediT roles where practical.
- Review is labelled exactly: editorial review, subject-matter review, methods review or external peer review. Mirellis never calls an internal check peer review.
- Authors, reviewers and editors disclose relevant employment, ownership, consulting, funding, personal and intellectual interests. A disclosed interest does not automatically disqualify a contribution; concealment does.
- Direct funding for the work is named separately from general institutional support.
- AI systems cannot be authors. Any material AI assistance is disclosed with the tool category and purpose. A human remains responsible for sources, wording, copyright and the final record.
- Unpublished submissions are confidential. They are not uploaded into third-party AI systems without the contributor's explicit permission and a suitable confidentiality arrangement.

## Editorial workflow

1. **Scope check:** Is the submission within sleep, focus, restoration or a named Human State Transition?
2. **Identity and disclosure check:** Verify authorship, contribution, funding, conflicts and AI use.
3. **Claim map:** Separate observation, interpretation, mechanism, intervention and product claims.
4. **Source check:** Verify every material source and record what it supports.
5. **Safety and privacy check:** Remove diagnosis, unsupported treatment language, identifiable private data and unsafe instructions.
6. **Editorial review:** Check clarity, proportionality, limitations, links and citation metadata.
7. **Decision:** Accept, invite revision, decline or hold. Submission never implies publication.
8. **Publication:** Assign a stable number, version, review date and correction route.
9. **Post-publication review:** Respond to material challenges and update status visibly.

## Corrections, updates and retirement

- Typographical changes that do not alter meaning may update the current version without a separate notice.
- A change that affects interpretation, evidence, credit or safety increments the version and adds a dated change note.
- A materially unreliable record is marked Under review or Retired. The original anchor remains resolvable with the notice and replacement link.
- Mirellis does not silently overwrite a material conclusion.
- Questions and correction requests go through `/pages/contact#contribute` and must identify the record number and challenged statement.

## Reference standards

This policy adapts, proportionately, principles from [COPE Core Practices](https://publicationethics.org/core-practices), [ICMJE recommendations on authorship, conflicts and AI use](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/), [Crossref guidance on versions and corrections](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/principles-practices/best-practices/versioning/) and the [CRediT contributor-role taxonomy](https://credit.niso.org/). Mirellis is not claiming membership, certification or formal peer-review equivalence with those bodies.
